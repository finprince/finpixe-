According to the provided documentation, Goods Receipt Notes (GRNs) record incoming materials and update stock levels and ledger entries as follows:

1. **Inward Quantity and Value**: When a GRN is recorded, the system updates the "Inward" quantity and value in the Stock Movement Summary dashboard under the Operations tab.
2. **Opening Stock**: The system also updates the "Opening Stock" quantity and value to reflect the new stock balance at the beginning of the period.
3. **Closing Stock**: The system calculates the "Closing Stock" by adding the "Inward" quantity and subtracting the "Outward" quantity, then updates the ledger entries accordingly.

Here's a step-by-step breakdown:

1. When a GRN is recorded, the system checks if there are any existing stock balances for the item.
2. If there are no existing stock balances, the system creates a new entry in the ledger with the "Inward" quantity and value.
3. If there are existing stock balances, the system updates the opening stock balance by adding the "Inward" quantity and subtracting the "Outward" quantity.
4. The system then calculates the closing stock balance by taking the sum of the updated opening stock balance and the "Inward" quantity minus the "Outward" quantity.
5. The calculated closing stock balance is then reflected in the ledger entries.

This process ensures that the inventory levels and ledger entries are accurately updated when a GRN is recorded, providing a real-time overview of stock flows and valuations.
