Based on the provided Evidence Documents, here are the steps for performing scrap inventory disposal:

**Scrap Disposed (Disposal)**

1. Select the SCRAP tab and check SCRAP DISPOSED.
2. Specify ISSUED FROM (production line location) and ISSUED TO (designated Scrap Yard).
3. Set PRODUCTION SLIP NO.: Select the relevant production slip from the dropdown to link the scrap to a specific batch run.
4. List the scrap items in the grid, specifying UOM, enter the Qty Generated, and click POST & CLOSE.

**Other Scrap (Dual Grid)**

1. Select the SCRAP tab and check OTHER SCRAP.
2. Specify numbering series, date, time, and locations.
3. Click + ADD ITEM to list the original commercial items written off in the "Items Scrapped" grid.
4. Click + ADD ITEM to log the recovered waste materials generated (along with quantity, rate, and scrap value) in the "Resulting Scrap" grid.
5. Click POST & CLOSE.

**Scrap Disposal Summary**

1. Select the SCRAP tab and check SCRAP DISPOSED.
2. Specify ISSUED FROM (production line location) and ISSUED TO (designated Scrap Yard).
3. PRODUCTION SLIP NO.: Select the relevant production slip from the dropdown to link the scrap to a specific batch run.
4. List the scrap items in the grid, specifying UOM, enter the Qty Generated, and click POST & CLOSE.

**Additional Steps**

1. Fill in disposal details:
	* REASON FOR DISPOSAL: Explanation note.
	* METHOD OF DISPOSAL: Process description (e.g., Recycling, Incineration).
2. Select the scrap buying contractor from the dropdown under DISPOSAL AGENCY.
3. Click UPLOAD CERTIFICATE to attach third-party disposal receipts or certificates.

By following these steps, you can accurately record and dispose of scrap inventory in your system.
