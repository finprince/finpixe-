Based on the provided Evidence Documents, the following reports are generated during Month-End Inventory Closing:

1. **Inventory Valuation Report**: This report provides an accurate valuation of inventory at the end of the month, considering the movement of goods, including receipts, issues, and scrap.
2. **Scrap Report**: This report details the quantity and value of scrap materials generated during the month, helping to identify areas for improvement in production processes.
3. **Consumption Slip Report**: This report provides a record of inventory items consumed internally, including those used for fixed assets or operations.
4. **Finished Goods Produced Report**: This report summarizes the quantity and value of finished goods produced during the month, providing insights into production performance.

These reports are essential for accurate financial reporting, inventory management, and decision-making in the organization.
