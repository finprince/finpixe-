Based on the provided evidence documents, serial numbers appear to be unique identifiers assigned to Goods Receipt Notes (GRN) and Issue Slips in an inventory management system.

According to the Step-by-Step guide for creating a numbering series, serial numbers are generated using a combination of prefix, suffix, digits, and start-from values. The prefix is characters at the beginning of the serial, the suffix is characters at the end, and the digits refer to the zero-padding length. For example, entering 4 as the digit value would generate serials like GRN-0001/2026.

The evidence also suggests that there are different types of serial numbers, including:

* GRN Series: This series appears to be used for Goods Receipt Notes (GRNs), which record incoming materials.
* Issue Slip Series: This series is used for issue slips, which likely track the movement of goods within the inventory system.

In addition, the evidence mentions that there are custom actions and endpoints related to serial numbers, such as:

* GET /api/master-voucher-grn/{id}/next-number/: This endpoint returns the next sequence number in a GRN series.
* GET /api/master-voucher-issue-slip/{id}/next-number/: This endpoint calculates the next serial number using a prefix counter check.

Overall, serial numbers appear to be an essential component of the inventory management system, allowing for tracking and identification of goods as they move through the system.
