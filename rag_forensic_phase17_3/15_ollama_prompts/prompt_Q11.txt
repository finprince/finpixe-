A.7 Recording Transactions: Issue Slips
Under Operations, click + ADD NEW ISSUE SLIP to open the slip entry form.
A.7.1 Job-Work Slips (Outward & Receipt)
Used for subcontractor operations where materials are sent to or returned from a job worker.
A. Goods Sent for Jobwork (Outward)
Select the JOB-WORK tab and choose GOODS SENT FOR JOBWORK.
Set the direction toggle to OUTWARD.
Choose the JOB-WORK SERIES template. The JOBWORK OUTWARD NO. will auto-populate.
---
Select the ISSUE SLIP SERIES (fills the ISSUE SLIP NO.).
Set transaction DATE, TIME, GOODS SENT FROM (source location) and GOODS SENT TO (destination location).
Add items in the grid, enter optional remarks under POSTING NOTE, and click POST & CLOSE.
A.7.4 Production Slips (Stages 1, 2, and 3)
Tracks materials through the manufacturing process.
Stage 1: Materials Issued for Production
Under the PRODUCTION tab, select MATERIALS ISSUED FOR PRODUCTION.
Select the ISSUE SLIP SERIES (generates the MATERIAL ISSUE SLIP NO.).

Step-by-Step: Creating a Location
