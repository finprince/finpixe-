A.2 Category Management (Stock Groups)
Inventory Categories let you organize items hierarchically. Finpixe pre-loads standard categories:
Raw Material: Unprocessed materials.
Work in Progress: Intermediate stages of production.
Finished Goods: Completed products ready for sale.
Stores and Spares: Operational and repair parts.
Packing Material: Materials used to package goods.
Stock in Trade: Goods purchased for direct resale.
By-product: Secondary products generated during manufacturing.
Scrap: Discarded material or waste.
---
Master: Configure basic records (categories, warehouses, stock items, and serial series templates).
Operations: Post transaction vouchers (Goods Receipt Notes and Issue Slips) and review stock movement dashboards.
To access the module, select INVENTORY from the left-hand navigation sidebar. Toggle between configurations and operations using the MASTER and OPERATIONS tabs at the top of the main screen.
