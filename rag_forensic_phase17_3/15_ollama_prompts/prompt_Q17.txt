SECTION A: End-User Functional Guide
This section is written for accountants, inventory managers, and warehouse operators to manage stock, locations, and transactions on a daily basis.
A.1 Module Overview & Navigation
The Finpixe Inventory module manages stock balances, godowns, categories, and transaction slips.
Master: Configure basic records (categories, warehouses, stock items, and serial series templates).
Operations: Post transaction vouchers (Goods Receipt Notes and Issue Slips) and review stock movement dashboards.
---
Inward (Qty & Value): Total units and values received via GRNs and inward operations.
Outward (Qty & Value): Total units and values dispatched via Issue Slips and outward operations.
Closing Stock (Qty & Value): Net physical stock remaining (Opening Qty + Inward Qty - Outward Qty) and its financial valuation.
Actions: Click View on any row to drill down into the transaction log (individual GRNs and Issue Slips) for that specific item.

A.7.2 Inter-Unit Slips
Used for inventory transfers between sister units or distinct corporate branches.
Select the INTER-UNIT tab.
Select the ISSUE SLIP SERIES to populate the ISSUE SLIP NO..
Set the transfer DATE and TIME.
Select GOODS SENT FROM (origin warehouse) and GOODS SENT TO (destination unit).
In the items grid, add the items (Code, Name, HSN/SAC, UOM, Qty, and Rate).
Fill in the Delivery Challan / Dispatch Details (transporter name, transport mode, vehicle registration, etc.) and click POST & CLOSE.

A.7.3 Location Change Slips
Used for internal stock movements between warehouses under the same company premises. It does not require transporter dispatch details or e-way bills.
Select the LOCATION CHANGE tab.
Select the ISSUE SLIP SERIES (fills the ISSUE SLIP NO.).
Set transaction DATE, TIME, GOODS SENT FROM (source location) and GOODS SENT TO (destination location).
---
Select the warehouse under ISSUED FROM (LOCATION).
Under ITEMS, click + ADD ITEM and enter Code, Name, UOM, Qty, and Rate. The system calculates the Capitalized Value automatically.
Click POST & CLOSE.
B. Issued for Daily Operations (Expensed)
Use this option when inventory is consumed for routine operations and should be expensed immediately.
Select the CONSUMPTION tab and check ISSUED FOR DAILY OPERATIONS.
Select the EXPENSE LEDGER* (e.g. Office Expenses) and specify the source warehouse location under ISSUED FROM (LOCATION).
