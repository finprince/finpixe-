A.2 Category Management (Stock Groups)
Inventory Categories let you organize items hierarchically. Finpixe pre-loads standard categories:
Raw Material: Unprocessed materials.
Work in Progress: Intermediate stages of production.
Finished Goods: Completed products ready for sale.
Stores and Spares: Operational and repair parts.
Packing Material: Materials used to package goods.
Stock in Trade: Goods purchased for direct resale.
By-product: Secondary products generated during manufacturing.
Scrap: Discarded material or waste.

A.7.7 Scrap Slips (Production, Reclaimed, & Disposal)
Scrap slips record unusable material wastage or log scrap sales.
A. Production Scrap
Used when scrap is generated during a specific production run.
Select the SCRAP tab and check PRODUCTION SCRAP.
Select the series (generates the SCRAP ISSUE SLIP NO.), DATE, and TIME.
Set ISSUED FROM (production line location) and ISSUED TO (designated Scrap Yard).
PRODUCTION SLIP NO.: Select the relevant production slip from the dropdown to link the scrap to a specific batch run.
---

List the scrap items in the grid, specify UOM, enter the Qty Generated, and click POST & CLOSE.
B. Other Scrap (Dual Grid)
Used when standard inventory items are damaged or expire, writing off the main product while recovering scrap materials.
Select the SCRAP tab and check OTHER SCRAP.
Specify numbering series, date, time, and locations.

FIXED ASSET LEDGER*: Select the target capital ledger account (e.g. Plant & Machinery) from the dropdown.
