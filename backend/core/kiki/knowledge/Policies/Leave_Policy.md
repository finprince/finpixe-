# FINPIXE Employee Leave Policy

## Leave Entitlements
All full-time employees are eligible for the following annual paid leave allocations per calendar year (January 1 to December 31):
- **Casual Leave (CL)**: 12 days per year.
- **Sick Leave (SL)**: 12 days per year (Medical certificate required for > 2 consecutive days).
- **Earned Leave / Annual Paid Leave (EL)**: 18 days per year.

## Leave Application Workflow:
1. Employee submits leave request via FINPIXE HR Portal at least 3 days in advance for casual leave.
2. Reporting Manager receives automated notification and approves/rejects request.
3. Unused Earned Leave can be carried forward up to a maximum limit of 30 days.
