# Double Entry Bookkeeping & Financial Accounting Principles

## Fundamentals of Debit and Credit
In double-entry bookkeeping, every financial transaction affects at least two accounts with equal and opposite entries.
- **Debit (Dr)**: Represents an increase in assets or expenses, or a decrease in liabilities, equity, or revenue. Debits are recorded on the left side of a ledger.
- **Credit (Cr)**: Represents an increase in liabilities, equity, or revenue, or a decrease in assets or expenses. Credits are recorded on the right side of a ledger.

### Golden Rules of Accounting:
1. **Personal Accounts**: Debit the Receiver, Credit the Giver.
2. **Real Accounts**: Debit what comes in, Credit what goes out.
3. **Nominal Accounts**: Debit all expenses & losses, Credit all incomes & gains.

## Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)
TDS is a statutory tax deduction mechanism under the Income Tax Act where the person responsible for making specified payments (such as rent, professional fees, contractor payments) deducts tax at source before remitting the net amount to the payee.
