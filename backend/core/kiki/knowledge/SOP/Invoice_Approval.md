# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) — Invoice Approval Workflow

## Purpose & Scope
This SOP defines authorization limits and approval workflows for supplier invoices and expense disbursements in FINPIXE ERP.

## Approval Authorization Matrix:
- **Up to ₹50,000**: Single approval by Accounts Payable Executive.
- **₹50,001 to ₹5,00,000**: Dual approval required by Department Head and Finance Manager.
- **Above ₹5,00,000**: Triple approval required by Chief Financial Officer (CFO) and Managing Director.

### Verification Checklist:
- 3-Way Match: Invoice amount must match PO price and GRN quantity.
- GSTIN verification on GST portal.
- Active vendor bank account verification.
