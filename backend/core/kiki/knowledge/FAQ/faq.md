# FINPIXE Enterprise AI & ERP Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: How do I file GSTR-3B in FINPIXE?
GSTR-3B can be generated under **GST -> GSTR-3B Tax Filing Report**. System auto-aggregates outward taxable supplies from sales orders and inward eligible Input Tax Credit (ITC) from purchase vouchers.

### Q2: What is the vendor onboarding process?
Vendors submit GSTIN, PAN, and Bank details. Accounts Payable verifies credentials and registers vendor master in FINPIXE ERP.

### Q3: What is Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)?
TDS is tax withheld on specified payments like professional services (194J) or rent (194I) before remitting payment to vendors.
