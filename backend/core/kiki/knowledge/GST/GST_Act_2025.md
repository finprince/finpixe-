# GST Act 2025 & Input Tax Credit Regulations

## Section 16 — Eligibility and Conditions for Taking Input Tax Credit (ITC)
Every registered person shall, subject to such conditions and restrictions as may be prescribed and in the manner specified in section 49, be entitled to take credit of input tax charged on any supply of goods or services or both to him which are used or intended to be used in the course or furtherance of his business.

### Conditions for Claiming Input Tax Credit:
1. The registered person is in possession of a tax invoice or debit note issued by a supplier registered under this Act.
2. The goods or services have been received by the registered person.
3. The tax charged in respect of such supply has been actually paid to the Government.
4. The registered person has furnished the return under Section 39 (GSTR-3B).

## Section 39 — GSTR-3B Monthly Return Filing Rules
Every registered person must file GSTR-3B on or before the 20th of the month following the relevant tax period. GSTR-3B summarizes outward supplies, input tax credit claimed, and net GST liability payable in cash.
