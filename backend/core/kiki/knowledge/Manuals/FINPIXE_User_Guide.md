# FINPIXE ERP System User Guide

## FINPIXE Purchase Workflow
The purchase lifecycle in FINPIXE ERP follows a structured 5-step process:
1. **Purchase Requisition (PR)**: Internal request initiated by a department.
2. **Purchase Order (PO)**: Formal order issued to an approved vendor specifying items, quantities, and agreed prices.
3. **Goods Receipt Note (GRN)**: Store keeper verifies physical goods inward against the PO.
4. **Vendor Invoice Entry**: Accounts Payable records the tax invoice received from the supplier.
5. **Payment Disbursement**: Finance approves voucher disbursement and updates vendor ledger balance.

## Vendor Onboarding Process
New vendors must submit GST Registration Certificate, PAN card copy, Bank Account Details, and MSME Certificate. Accounts Payable verifies details before creating vendor master record in FINPIXE ERP.
