Serial Number Tracking Procedure:

The Serial Number Tracking Procedure involves configuring serial series templates in the Finpixe Inventory module. This allows users to generate unique serial numbers for tracking individual items, such as products or components.

To configure serial number tracking, follow these steps:

1. Go to Inventory > Master and click on the "Serial Series Templates" tab.
2. Click + ADD NEW SERIAL SERIES TEMPLATE.
3. Fill out the template details:
	* Digits*: Zero-padding length (e.g., entering 4 generates serials like 0001).
	* Start From*: Starting sequence number (e.g., 1).
4. Review the dynamically updated example (e.g., GRN-0001/2026) in the Sample Preview.
5. Click SAVE to save the template.

Once a serial series template is configured, users can generate unique serial numbers for tracking individual items. This feature helps maintain an accurate inventory audit trail and ensures that all transactions are properly recorded and tracked.
