Based on the provided Evidence Documents, I can provide a step-by-step guide on how to integrate Multi-Location Warehouse Management with Purchase Orders.

**Step 1: Create Locations**

To enable multi-location warehouse management, you need to create separate locations for each warehouse or office. To do this:

* Open Inventory > Master > Location.
* Fill out the Create Location form:
	+ Location Name*: Name of the warehouse or office (e.g., Chennai Main Warehouse).
	+ GROUP input box: Type the group name (e.g., Import) and click CREATE GROUP.

**Step 2: Assign Locations to Purchase Orders**

Once you have created locations, you can assign them to purchase orders. To do this:

* Open Purchase Order > Create Purchase Order.
* Fill out the Create Purchase Order form:
	+ Vendor Name & Branch: Select the supplier (for purchases) or customer (for sales returns).
	+ GSTIN and address details will auto-fill.
	+ Location: Select the receiving company warehouse from the dropdown list.

**Step 3: Upload Documents**

After assigning locations to purchase orders, you can upload relevant documents such as invoices or delivery challans. To do this:

* Open Purchase Order > Create Purchase Order.
* Fill out the Create Purchase Order form:
	+ Upload Document: Click to attach the supplier's invoice or delivery challan.

**Step 4: Track Stock Across Locations**

Once you have created locations and assigned them to purchase orders, you can track stock across different company premises or third-party facilities. To do this:

* Open Inventory > Master > Location.
* Select a location from the category tree.
* View the stock levels for that location.

By following these steps, you can integrate Multi-Location Warehouse Management with Purchase Orders and effectively manage your inventory across different locations.
